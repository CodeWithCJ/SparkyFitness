# App Shell And Navigation: details

Longer notes for the "App Shell And Navigation" section of [`SparkyFitnessMobile/AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md). Each one has a one-line link there.

## Quick Actions sheet (AddSheet)

- `AddSheet` is the Quick Actions sheet: a title with Done, four tinted tiles in one panel (Food, Exercise, Measure, Scan by default; Exercise opens a tinted list of Workout / Activity / Log Workout / Record Activity), a list panel of chevron rows (Daily Check-In, Progress Photos, the cycle row (Wellness, Log Cycle or Log Pregnancy Entry while cycle tracking is on), Symptoms & Health), compact Mood and Mindfulness pairs, and Ask Sparky and Sync Health as buttons at the foot. How each item looks below the tiles is `ADD_MENU_ITEM_ROW_STYLES`, its tile colour `ADD_MENU_ITEM_TINTS` (`constants/addMenuItems.ts`); `AddMenuIconTile` draws the tile for the sheet and its settings. Check-In is the guided check-in: it chains `MoodLog` → `MeasurementsAdd` → `ProgressPhotos` for one day through the optional `guidedCheckIn` route param (`GuidedCheckInParams`; `hooks/useGuidedCheckIn.ts`, `components/GuidedCheckInStepIndicator.tsx`). The handler fixes the day once (the Diary's active date, else the diary store's), and every hop is a `replace` guarded by `isFocused()` and a one-shot latch, so a save that resolves after the user left never moves the stack. Each step shows "Check-in: Step N of 3"; steps 1 and 2 have Skip and "Save & continue", and steps 2 and 3 have a Back in the same bar that replaces the step with the one before (disabled while a save or a photo is pending). Skip and Back ask first when the step has edits: on `MeasurementsAdd` when the form differs from what the day holds (a value typed and cleared again is not an edit), on the mood forms once any field has been touched. Every step saves on its own, so going back drops nothing that was saved, and `MeasurementsAdd` remembers its own writes (`stepSavedRef`) so a retry after a partial failure, Skip or Back still reports the step as saved. "Save & continue" moves on without writing on `MeasurementsAdd` when nothing differs from the day's values and on the one-entry mood form while it is untouched (so an untouched form never saves its default mood); the multi-check-in composer keeps it disabled until the check-in has content, leaving an empty step to Skip. Steps 2 and 3 lock the date (no `CalendarSheet`, so the global diary date is untouched). All three steps are sheets: `ProgressPhotos` is pushed on its own, but its route options in `App.tsx` add `presentation: 'modal'` when `route.params.guidedCheckIn` is set, and then it pads the top inset like a modal (Android only). The steps keep to logging the day: inside the flow `ProgressPhotos` leaves out Compare and Time-lapse (its capped history note no longer points at them) and the Log weight prompts (`PhotoDayWeight` without `onLogWeight` shows only a stored weight), since step 2 logs the weight and those prompts open a `MeasurementsAdd` with a date picker. Step 3 has a Close X and a header Finish, both disabled while a picked photo is processed, uploaded or deleted. Photos save as they are added, so on step 3 the X, Finish, system Back and the iOS swipe down all finish the flow (the last two through a `beforeRemove` listener calling `onNavigatorClose`; while a picked photo is still on its way it waits until the photo has landed or failed): it goes back, shows "Check-in complete" when a step saved something and, after a new mood check-in, makes the one-time reminder offer that `MoodLog` otherwise makes on leaving. Leaving at steps 1-2 (X, system Back, a swipe) keeps what was saved, without the toast or the offer; the step-bar Back never leaves the flow (on step 3 it returns to step 2 carrying whether a photo was saved). A `checkinId` from elsewhere turns the flow off; the flow only edits the check-in its own step 1 created, which it carries as `guidedCheckIn.moodCheckin` (id and the day it was saved on) so going back to step 1 opens it for editing instead of adding another (no Delete there, an unchanged edit moves on without writing, and a check-in deleted meanwhile is replaced by a new one, decided only once the day's list has finished loading or refreshing, since a list cached before step 1 created the check-in lacks it; a failed load shows Retry rather than a Back that would close the flow). On an older one-entry server step 1 saves over the day's mood, as the standalone form does. Each hop also records the check-in in progress (day, step to open, flags) in `stores/guidedCheckInStore.ts`, a device-local persisted store cleared when step 3 finishes and on an account or server switch (`useAuth`'s identity-change reset); Check-In in the Add menu, and the What's New check-in card's "Start a check-in" for the device's today (`getTodayDate`), both go through `startGuidedCheckIn`, which asks "Continue your check-in?" when one was left on the same day, with Continue (opens the recorded step) and Start over (clears it; a new mood check-in is added rather than the earlier one edited). Its Mood row opens `MoodLog`, and the Dashboard has a toggleable `mood` card (today's mood, 7-day trend) that opens it (mood faces, tags, a Journal box, and a mood-over-time chart). `useMoodCheckinsSupport` probes `GET /api/mood/date/<today>` with `X-Mood-Model-Version: 2` the first time the `mood` card, `MoodLog` or `MoodDay` mounts, and the answer is cached for the session: an array back means the server takes several check-ins a day and any other answer keeps the one-entry-per-day screen via `POST /api/mood`. A failed probe with nothing known shows a Retry on `MoodLog` and `MoodDay` (`components/mood/MoodSupportCheckFailed`) rather than either form, because the one-entry POST on a v2 server edits the day's latest check-in instead of adding one. On a v2 server the `mood` card shows one mood per day (the average of your own moods, else the Garmin or imported one), its body always opens `MoodDay` (the day's check-ins in time order with the day's averages; tap to edit in `MoodLog`, swipe or long-press to delete, Add check-in for a new one), which replaces itself with `MoodLog` for the day on an older server, and its "+ Log" (shown when no mood is logged) opens `MoodLog` for a new check-in. `MoodLog` takes an optional `checkinId` to edit; a new check-in for today goes on the profile zone's today even when the dashboard passed the device's day (`utils/moodDay.ts` `moodDayInZone`, which `MoodDay` also uses so its list shows that day; its redirect to the one-entry form keeps the requested day); each face writes the shared `moodFaceValue` (Sad 10 to Excited 90, as on the web); every field is optional but at least one is required, the mood can be left unset (tapping the selected face clears it), the time row opens `TimeSheet` and stays on the current time until set, and edit mode has Delete check-in. The mood report and history chart use one value per day. It also has optional 1-5 Energy and Focus taps (`components/mood/RatingSelector`) that are sent only when changed on the screen (absent means keep, `null` means clear); `useMoodRatingsSupport` applies only to the one-entry-per-day form; it hides them when the server's mood rows lack `energy_value` and shows them when there are no rows to tell, and a save whose response lacks the key shows a one-time "update your server" toast. Its Progress Photos row opens `ProgressPhotos` on the diary's active date. Its Mindfulness row opens `MindfulnessLog` (`MindfulnessLogScreen`), a root-stack modal laid out like `MoodLog` and `MeasurementsAdd` (screen header, scrolling form, sticky `FooterSaveBar`, so the navigator owns Android's Back); `MindfulnessDetail` opens the same screen from the Log Mindful Session row on its Practice & Log tab and, with a `session` param, from Edit on its History tab, so it has no log form of its own. Session type names and icons come from `utils/mindfulnessSessionTypes.ts`, shared by the log screen, the detail screen and `MindfulnessCard`; the list is the shared `mindfulnessSessionTypeSchema`, and a stored type it does not have shows as Other and is kept on save. Paced Breathing and the Meditation Timer keep Finish & Save on screen while the save runs, so they go through `useSaveOncePerRun`: a run is saved once (presses are dropped until the practice is reset, taps replayed before that reset renders included), a failed save can be retried, and a save from a run closed while pending does not reset the next one. `MindfulnessLog` goes back after a save only while it is still focused, because it can be opened from a screen other than the tab host. Keep `AddSheet`'s present/dismiss refs intact to avoid Android re-present loops. Android's hardware Back in `AddSheet` does what the sheet's own Back link does: from the Exercise menu it returns to the main menu, from the main menu it closes the sheet.
