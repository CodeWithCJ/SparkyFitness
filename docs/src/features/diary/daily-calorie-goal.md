# Daily Calorie Goal

SparkyFitness allows you to set and track your daily calorie goals, providing insights into your nutritional intake. Details on how to configure and monitor your calorie goals will be added in the future.

## Training and rest days

With [calorie cycling](/features/settings/calculation-settings#calorie-cycling) on, the goal changes by weekday: the Diary shows **High day +210** or **Low day −160** next to it, and **How today's target is calculated** includes the cycling step. Carbs take the change and protein stays the same.

## How today's target is calculated

Under the goal, **How today's target is calculated** shows the working as numbered steps: **1** resting metabolism, **2** body fat, **3** adaptive TDEE (when the adaptive method is used) and the **daily calorie goal** last. Each step shows its result; open it to see the formula, the data it used and any safety-floor notes. In Calculation Settings the steps are shown open under the Live Preview.
