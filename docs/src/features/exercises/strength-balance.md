# Strength Balance

Strength Balance shows how your main lifts compare with each other and with your body weight. It appears on the web **Reports → Exercise** page, under the snapshot charts, and on the mobile **Exercise statistics** screen in the Strength view, once you have logged any strength sets.

## What it shows

- **Lifetime totals:** total volume lifted, working sets and sessions since your first logged strength set. Warm-up sets are left out.
- **Key lifts:** your best estimated one-rep max for the squat, bench press, deadlift, overhead press and row. The estimate uses the Epley formula on your best logged set. When several exercises count as the same lift (for example "Back Squat" and "Barbell Squat"), the strongest one is shown. Front squats, Romanian deadlifts and incline bench presses are kept out, because they are not the same movement.
- **Times body weight:** each key lift as a multiple of your latest logged body weight. Log a weight check-in to see it.
- **Ratios:** how pairs of lifts compare, with a commonly used range for each:

| Ratio | Usual range |
| --- | --- |
| Bench press / Squat | 0.65 to 0.85 |
| Deadlift / Squat | 1.10 to 1.30 |
| Overhead press / Bench press | 0.55 to 0.75 |
| Row / Bench press | 0.70 to 1.00 |

A ratio outside its range is marked **Below range** or **Above range**. It is a hint about which lift may be lagging, not a rule; body proportions and training goals change what is normal for you.

## Untrained muscles

The **Muscle Heat Map** has a **Trained / Untrained** switch. **Untrained** flips the colours: muscles with no working sets in the selected range are highlighted, so the ones you are skipping stand out.

## Turning it off

On mobile, hide the card from **Settings → Reports**, under the Exercise sections.

## Older servers

The card needs a server that has the strength summary. With an older server the card stays hidden and nothing else changes. The **Untrained** switch works with any server.
