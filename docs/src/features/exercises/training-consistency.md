# Training Consistency

A view of how regularly you train, built from the workouts you have already logged. There is nothing to set up.

## Where to find it

- **Mobile:** Exercise Statistics, under the muscle heat map. Reach it from the Dashboard's exercise progress card or from the Library.
- **Web:** Reports → Exercise, in the left column under your key stats.

## What it shows

- **Week streak.** The number of weeks in a row, with at least one logged workout. A week that is still in progress does not break the streak: until you train this week, the streak counts back from last week.
- **Longest.** Your longest run of such weeks within the last 26 weeks.
- **This week.** Distinct days you trained this week.
- **Training calendar (mobile).** One column per week, with the weekday names down the side, month names above, and a filled square for each day you trained. Weeks start on the first day of the week you chose in your preferences (Sunday if you have not chosen one). The web Reports page already has a twelve-month workout calendar higher up the page, and shows a bar for each of the last 12 weeks here.
- **Sets per muscle.** Working sets for each primary muscle this week against last week. Warm-up sets are not counted, and a set counts for every primary muscle of its exercise, the same way the muscle heat map counts them.

## Good to know

- A week runs from your chosen first day of the week. Changing that setting regroups the weeks, so the streak can change with it.
- The window is fixed at the last 26 weeks and does not follow the 7/30/90-day range control.
- Weeks and days use your profile timezone.
- Calorie summaries synced from a device (for example Apple Health "Active Calories") are not workouts and are left out.
