# Shoe Inventory

Keep a list of your running shoes and see how many kilometres (or miles) each pair has done, so you know when it is time to replace them.

---

## Adding a pair

1. Open the **Equipment** section: on the web it is at the bottom of the **Exercises** page; in the mobile app it is **Library > Equipment**.
2. Tap **Add shoe** and fill in the details:
   - **Name** is the only required field. Brand, model and a nickname are optional.
   - **Starting mileage** is for a pair that already has some distance on it when you add it.
   - **Retire at** is the distance after which the pair counts as worn out. It starts at 800 km (about 500 mi).
   - **Purchase date** and **Price** are optional.
3. Switch on **Use as my default pair** to have new runs recorded in the mobile app, outdoor (GPS) or indoor, logged with that pair. Only one pair can be the default; choosing another one replaces it.

Distances follow your distance unit (kilometres or miles). They are stored in kilometres, so changing the unit later converts them.

## Linking a run to a pair

- **Web:** open a cardio entry (a run, walk or ride) from your diary, pick the shoe under **Shoe**, and save.
- **Mobile:** open the run from your diary (or from **Exercise Statistics**) and use **Shoe**. A run recorded in the app, outdoor or indoor, is saved with your default pair automatically; change it from the session screen if you wore a different one.

A pair's mileage is its starting mileage plus the distance of every run linked to it. Changing or removing the link, editing the distance, or deleting the run updates the total.

## Wear and retirement

Each card shows a progress bar, the number of runs and the date of the last run. Once the total reaches the **Retire at** distance the card shows a **Worn out** badge and asks whether to retire the pair, with a one-tap **Retire** button.

**Retire** moves a pair to the retired list and clears its default flag. Its past runs keep their link, so the mileage history stays intact, and you can **Reactivate** it later. A retired pair no longer appears in the shoe picker unless a run is already linked to it.

**Delete** removes the pair. Runs logged with it stay in your diary but are no longer linked to a shoe.

## Family access

Shoes are part of the exercise diary. A family member with **Manage Diary** access can add pairs and link runs on your behalf; **View Reports** access can see them.
