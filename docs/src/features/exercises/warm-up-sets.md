# Warm-Up Sets

Add a ramp of warm-up sets to a lift in one tap, instead of typing each one in between sets.

## Add warm-ups

On the mobile app, open the **…** menu on an exercise and choose **Add warm-ups**. It works in an active workout, and also while you are editing a workout preset, a new workout or a logged workout. The sets are built from the weight of the exercise's first working set, whether you typed it or it is the suggested weight shown in grey.

By default the ramp is:

| Set | Weight | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 40% of the working weight | 5 |
| 2 | 60% | 5 |
| 3 | 80% | 3 |

Weights are rounded to a loadable step in your unit. A step that would not climb above the one before it, or that reaches the working weight, is left out, so a lighter lift gets a shorter ramp.

## Settings

Under **Settings → Workout Settings → Warm-up calculator**:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Warm-up Calculator** | Turns the **Add warm-ups** menu item on or off. |
| **Warmup Method** | The ramp itself: each warm-up set is a percent of the working weight for a number of reps. **Add set** and **Remove set** change its length (up to eight sets), and **Reset to default** brings back the ramp above. Sets are kept in order from lightest to heaviest. |
| **Plate rounding** | The step warm-up weights round to on a bar (2.5 kg or 5 lb by default). |
| **Dumbbell rounding** | The step used for exercises that use dumbbells (1 kg or 2 lb by default). |

## How the sets behave

- They use the **Warm-up** set type, so they stay out of volume, personal records and progression, the same as warm-ups you add by hand. They stay editable, and you log them like any other set.
- Each has a 60-second rest.
- Tapping **Add warm-ups** again replaces warm-ups you have not logged yet instead of adding more. Once you have logged a warm-up, the option is hidden for that exercise.
- The option is not shown for cardio or timed exercises, or when the first working set has no weight yet.
- In an active workout, a warm-up row's **Prev** value comes from your last warm-up of that exercise, and a working set's comes from your last working set.
