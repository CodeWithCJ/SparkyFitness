# Features Index

SparkyFitness tracks the parts of your life that move your body and your health: what you eat and drink, how you train, how you sleep, and how you feel. This page is a map of the features, organized by what you want to do.

> [!TIP]
> New here? Start with [Goals](/features/goals) to set your daily targets, then log your day in the [Daily Diary](/features/diary/meals) and [Check-In](/features/check-in).

## Log your day

- [Daily Diary](/features/diary/meals): meals and food entries, with meal plans and meal presets
- [Water Intake](/features/diary/water-intake): hydration card with containers and quick-add drinks
- [NFC Tags](/features/nfc-tags): tap a tag on a bottle to log a drink
- [Nutrition Summary](/features/diary/nutrition-summary): your daily totals against your goals, plus 14-day trends
- [Daily Calorie Goal](/features/diary/daily-calorie-goal): the energy goal card: eaten, burned, and how you're tracking
- [Exercise Diary](/features/diary/exercise): log workouts, cardio, and strength sets
- [Check-In](/features/check-in): measurements, fasting & mood, mindfulness, sleep, progress photos, and symptoms
- [Symptom & Episode Tracking](/features/symptom-tracking): track migraines, pain, flare-ups, and other episodes

## Set your goals

- [Goals](/features/goals): daily calorie and macro goals, goal weight, goal presets, and weekly goal plans
- [Daily Calorie Goal](/features/diary/daily-calorie-goal): what the goal card on the Diary shows
- [Calculation Settings](/features/settings/calculation-settings): goal modes, TDEE formulas, and adaptive targets

## See your progress

- [Reports](/features/reports): charts, tables, and exports for nutrition, water, exercise, measurements, sleep, fasting, and more
- [Measurements](/features/measurements): log body metrics and custom categories, then watch the trends
- [Training Consistency](/features/exercises/training-consistency): how regularly you train
- [Cycle Hub](/features/cycle-hub/): cycle tracking, trying-to-conceive, and pregnancy modes

## Manage your food & exercise library

- [Searching](/features/searching): how search works everywhere in the app
- [Food Search](/features/food/food-search): find foods from your library, online providers, or a barcode scan
- [Food Database Manager](/features/food/food-database-manager): your foods, meals, and meal plans in one table
- [Custom Food Form](/features/food/custom-food-form): create foods from scratch
- [Feature Comparison](/features/comparison): what each food data provider offers
- [Exercise Search](/features/exercises/exercise-search): find exercises from your library or online
- [Exercise Database Manager](/features/exercises/exercise-database-manager): your exercises, workout presets, and workout plans

## Train smarter

- [Interval & WOD Workouts](/features/exercises/interval-wod-workouts): interval timers, circuits, and CrossFit-style scoring
- [Guided Workouts](/features/exercises/guided-workouts): coached workout playback
- [Progression & Per-Set Ramp](/features/exercises/progression-and-per-set-ramp): progressive overload and per-set ramps
- [Adaptive Coaching](/features/exercises/adaptive-coaching): suggested weights based on your history
- [Bodyweight Exercises](/features/exercises/bodyweight-exercises): weighted and assisted bodyweight sets
- [Warm-Up Sets](/features/exercises/warm-up-sets): add a warm-up ramp to a lift in one tap

## Connect & share

- [Family & Friends Sharing](/features/family-friends-sharing): invite family and friends with granular permissions
- [Sharing](/features/sharing): export your data, create API keys, and connect external apps
- [MCP Server](/features/mcp-server): let AI assistants read and write your data
- [AI Nutrition Assistant](/features/ai-assistant): Sparky Buddy for photo logging and questions
- [External Providers](/features/settings/external-providers): food, exercise, and wearable integrations
- [Canadian Nutrient File](/features/settings/canadian-nutrient-file): Health Canada's free food composition database

## Personalize

- [User Settings](/features/user-settings): everything in the Settings page, tab by tab
- [Preferences](/features/settings/preferences): units, date formats, timezone, and target bedtime
- [Nutrient Display](/features/settings/nutrient-display-settings): choose which nutrients appear where
- [Login Management](/features/settings/login-management): email, password, passkeys, and MFA
